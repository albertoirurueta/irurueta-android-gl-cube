= Sample app

NOTE: This documentation was generated with the assistance of AI. Please report any inaccuracies.

The repository's `app/` module is a minimal application (`com.irurueta.android.gl.cube.app`) that depends on the
`lib` module through `implementation(project(":lib"))`. It has a single activity showing a large text with a
full-screen `CubeTextureView` on top of it. The demo animation on the xref:index.adoc[overview page] was recorded
with it.

== Layout

`app/src/main/res/layout/activity_main.xml` declares a `TextView` followed by the cube view, so the text is drawn
behind the cube and remains visible through its transparent background:

[source,xml]
----
<androidx.constraintlayout.widget.ConstraintLayout xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android"
    xmlns:app="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res-auto"
    xmlns:tools="http://schemas.android.com/tools"
    android:layout_width="match_parent"
    android:layout_height="match_parent"
    tools:context="com.irurueta.android.gl.cube.MainActivity">

    <TextView
        android:layout_width="wrap_content"
        android:layout_height="wrap_content"
        android:text="@string/lorem_ipsum"
        android:textSize="40sp"
        android:textStyle="bold"
        app:layout_constraintLeft_toLeftOf="parent"
        app:layout_constraintRight_toRightOf="parent"
        app:layout_constraintTop_toTopOf="parent" />

    <com.irurueta.android.gl.cube.CubeTextureView
        android:id="@+id/cube"
        android:layout_width="match_parent"
        android:layout_height="match_parent"/>

</androidx.constraintlayout.widget.ConstraintLayout>
----

== Activity

`app/src/main/java/com/irurueta/android/gl/cube/MainActivity.kt`:

. In `onCreate`, it sets an `OnSurfaceChangedListener` that halves the cube size, sets its initial rotation and
  creates a `PinholeCamera` from the projection matrix of a Pixel 2 device (see xref:usage.adoc#_creating_a_camera[Creating a camera]).
. It builds two animators, played together with an `AnimatorSet`:
** a rotation animator that changes the cube Euler angles linearly, completing 3, 2 and 1 full turns around each axis
   every 30 seconds;
** a skewness animator that oscillates the camera skewness between -1000 and 1000 every second.
. `onResume` and `onPause` forward the lifecycle to the view and start or pause the animators.

See xref:animation.adoc[Animation] for the code.
